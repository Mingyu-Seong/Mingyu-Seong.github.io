Mingyu Seong

Gait control, assistive exoskeletons, wearable robotics

I dream of a day when everyone in the world is happier. I hope the robots I build help us get there.

I am a master's graduate in Robotics Engineering from Yeungnam University, where I worked with Prof. Jungsu Choi in the Human Robot Convergence Laboratory. I design wearable assistive robots, and I build every layer of them.

I am applying for PhD study starting Fall 2027. Control of assistive devices is the work I want to do, and I bring to it something uncommon on that side of the field: I can build the device the controller runs on.

I am glad to talk about assistive device control, or to send the full thesis and device videos. Based in Gyeongsan, Republic of Korea (KST, UTC+9).

Research

Robotic Achilles Tendon

An ankle assist with no motor and no microcontroller: an air spring that one foot sensor locks during stance and frees during swing. I designed it from the mechanism to the circuit board and tested it with six participants.
