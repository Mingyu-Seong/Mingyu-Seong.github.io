ICCAS 2025

Incheon, Korea · November 2025 First author Oral

Reading the gait phase from one wrist-worn IMU. Arm and leg swing stay in step as the body conserves angular momentum, so a single sensor on the wrist can time the assistance, with no complex algorithm or high-end processor. I closed with how that IMU sensing should improve next.

IROS 2025

Hangzhou, China · October 2025 First author Oral Poster

A semi-passive actuator for the Robotic Achilles Tendon. A double-acting pneumatic cylinder becomes a spring when its valve closes in stance and lets the ankle move freely when it opens in swing, so the device assists without a motor.

Joint IFAC MECHATRONICS / ROBOTICS Symposium

Paris, France · July 2025 First author Oral

Letting the ground reaction force decide when the spring engages. The wearer’s own ground reaction force, carried through an incompressible liquid, switches an origami-structured spring on in stance and off in swing. I closed with where we were taking it next: a fully unpowered flow-blocking system.

KRoC 2025

Pyeongchang, Korea · February 2025 First author Poster

The flow-blocking idea, and first signs that it is feasible. A spring that engages only while the airflow in a closed circuit is blocked, timed to the stance phase from foot-flat to toe-off. Early measurements showed the assist force rising linearly with piston displacement.

ICROS 2024

Daejeon, Korea · July 2024 First author Oral Outstanding Paper Award

A force transmitter modeled on the human sarcomere. A fluid-filled bellows transmits the force. Rings modeled on the Z-disk hold its shape under load, and an inner tube that slides against them, after the sliding filament theory, keeps it from twisting.
